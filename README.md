# Floating Isles Showcase

You are a professional web designer creating a modern, minimal static website for a company that manufactures and sells floating island ecosystems. These are innovative floating landscapes composed of roots, vegetation, and soil that naturally float on water surfaces without being anchored to the bottom, and can be deployed to lakes and bodies of water worldwide.

## Brand & Visual Direction
- Incorporate the company logo prominently in the header
- Design language: Minimal, modern, clean aesthetic
- Color scheme: Build the entire design around the logo colors and aesthetic, add other colors to the page that fit with the logo color
- Typography: Use modern, sans-serif fonts for a contemporary feel
- Spacing: Generous whitespace to maintain a premium, uncluttered look
- Approach: Let the logo guide the visual hierarchy and color palette

## Website Structure & Content

### Header/Navigation
- website language: Romanian
- Sticky header with logo on the left
- Company email address displayed prominently in header or top right
- Company phone number displayed in header

### Hero Section
- Full-width video background (autoplay, not muted, looped)
- video: https://youtu.be/lzDjzQGUcMI?feature=shared
- Video should showcase the floating islands in action or in nature
- Overlay text (optional): Brief tagline or company name
- Ensure video autoplays when users enter the site
- Video should be responsive and work on all devices

### Main Content Areas (choose layout that feels minimal and modern)

**Section 1: What We Do**
- Short description of floating island ecosystems
- Explain: These are innovative floating landscapes made of roots, vegetation, and soil
- They naturally float on water surfaces
- No anchoring required to the lake bottom
- They can be deployed to any lake or body of water

**Section 2: Why Choose Us**
- 2-3 key benefits (minimal bullet points or cards)
- Examples: Sustainability, biodiversity, aesthetic enhancement, water management

**Section 3: Contact/CTA**
- Email address: [user will provide]
- Phone number: [user will provide]
- Simple contact form or direct email/phone links
- Call-to-action button encouraging inquiry

## Technical Requirements
- Fully responsive design (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Fast loading performance
- Clean, semantic HTML
- Modern CSS with smooth animations (subtle)
- Autoplay video on page load
- Accessible color contrast and readable text

## Design Tone
- Premium and innovative
- Environmentally conscious
- Modern yet natural
- Professional but approachable
- Focus on visual storytelling with the video as the hero

## Additional Notes
- Keep the design single-page or multi-section scrolling
- Ensure all text is legible over any background
- Use the logo as the primary design anchor
- Minimize text, let visuals speak
- Ensure email and phone are clickable (mailto: and tel: links)
- The site should feel like a luxury product showcase
